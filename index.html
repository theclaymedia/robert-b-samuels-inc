Samuels DATACOM

Telecommunications infrastructure, in-house

Our Telecommunication Infrastructure division was established in 2002 through the purchase of AR Communications Corporation. Together, the company is a diversified communication group specializing in voice, data, alarm, control, and fiber-video cabling infrastructure, with RCDD personnel on staff to design cable plant.

We offer a complete line of TIA/EIA-568A/B compliant hardware — Category 6A UTP, coaxial and fiber optic — and are certified installers backed by manufacturer warranties. Every system is tested with Fluke copper and fiber test sets; for demanding requirements we field OTDR and fusion-splicing equipment for loose-tube, ribbon, single- and multi-mode fiber.

A Project Manager is assigned to every job, large or small, responsible for scheduling, change orders, reporting and overall site management — with schedules shared directly with clients.
